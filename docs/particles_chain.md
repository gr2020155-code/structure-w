# The particles chain: photon, neutrino and the pre-material level

28 November 2026 · Theory: Hrachya Danielyan

This file translates the matrix records on the photon, the neutrino and the level below matter. It is a reading aid. The authoritative text is the Russian matrix [`system/matrix.json`](../system/matrix.json). If the two differ, the matrix is right.

## How to read

- **IDs.** In the matrix, IDs use Cyrillic letters. Here they are written in Latin: А = A (author's postulate), Д = D (data), Р = R (calculation), В = V (statement), С = S (withdrawn), Х = X (contradiction).
- **Status** of a statement:
  - *derived*: follows from the named postulates without extra assumptions;
  - *computed*: output of a program whose rules were committed before it ran;
  - *candidate*: agrees with data, not proven;
  - *open*.
- **Data rule.** Only measured data count as a check. A result of official theory is used only as a "bridge, model".
- **Fit rule.** A choice first seen in the data is not a fit once it is explained from the postulates. It is still disclosed.
- **Words for W objects:**
  - *point*: the minimal discrete unit;
  - *loop* (circle): the minimal self-closure;
  - *figure-eight*: two loops of opposite orientation, a local self-closed form — matter (A-99, A-106);
  - *tick*: one change (A-01);
  - *global figure-eight*: the path of a point through the whole (A-47);
  - *lag*: falling behind one's global figure-eight; this lag is rest mass (V-212).

## Postulates

**A-18 (author).** A point is a self-closure by one loop. A neutrino is a self-closure by a figure-eight. Whether it is ν or ν̄ is set by the decay in which it was born.

**A-19 (author; extended 28.11).** A photon is a point without the self-closure of a particle. It moves one step per tick, and each step is an exchange with a point of the medium.

The extension (author, 28.11): the photon's point is itself self-closed (A-18). To have distinguishability ("I am"), a point must be self-closed. The minimal self-closure is a circle, and in the photon it lies in the point itself, the minimal discrete unit. What the photon lacks is only the closure of a particle: a figure-eight and a circuit.

**A-109 (author, 28.11).** Every point, the photon included, has the minimal possible "mass", a unit of being: 1 unit of "mass" = 1 tick = 1 step. This is why the speed of information (1 tick, 1 step) is finite. The unit is the same for all points.

Rest mass is the lag behind the global figure-eight on top of this unit (V-212):
- zero for a plain point and for the photon;
- very small for a pre-material link;
- the particle mass for figure-eights.

The check made before recording:
- If this unit were a rest mass of the photon, the photon's speed would depend on frequency. That contradicts A-19 and V-45. Data: no frequency dependence of the speed of light is seen; m_γ < 10⁻¹⁸ eV (PDG).
- As a unit common to all points, it contradicts nothing. A uniform background of identical units does not act as a difference, so it does not conflict with the clustering requirement V-398.

## Statements

**V-46 (derived; refined 28.11).** The photon has no rest mass: it has no circuit of a particle and does not lag behind its global figure-eight. It does have the unit of being (A-109), like any point.

**V-47 (derived; refined 28.11).** The photon's spin is not half-integer. It has no self-closure of a particle (no figure-eight) and so no 2:1 rule. It has only the closure of the point itself by a loop (A-18, A-19).

**V-417 (derived; from A-60, V-211; magnitude by A-04).** The photon differs from a plain point of the medium by its tempo difference from the global figure-eight, which is its energy.
1. Energy is the tempo difference between a self-closed object and the exchanges of the global figure-eight (A-60, V-211). The photon's point is self-closed (A-18, A-19), so A-60 applies to it.
2. A point of the medium makes one exchange per tick and goes along its own global figure-eight (V-211). There is no difference, so its energy is zero. This defines a "plain" point.
3. The photon has a difference, and that is its energy. It has no particle closure and no lag, so its rest mass is zero (V-46). All its energy is transfer: E = pc (V-209), with magnitude E = ħω (A-04).
4. Open (V-49): what sets the frequency of a point, that is, the size of the tempo difference; and why the photon's angular momentum is ħ.

**V-416 (candidate; author: "this will become a derivation later").** A loop (circle) is pre-material. Its mass is non-zero but very small. Matter is figure-eights. There are no glueballs as heavy particles.
1. **From the postulates.** Matter is local figure-eights, and there is also a pre-material level of links (A-99, A-106). The axis transfer, the analogue of a gluon, is a closed complement loop (V-352, V-355), so it is pre-material. The photon is a point closed in itself (A-18, A-19). Its closure lies within one discrete unit, so it has no circuit and no lag: its rest mass is zero and there is no dispersion (V-45, V-46, V-417). A pre-material link closes over several points; the photon closes in the point itself.
2. **Mass of a closed loop (author).** Non-zero but very small: closure gives mass (V-209).
   - Gluon: a mass up to a few MeV is not excluded by data (PDG).
   - Photon: m_γ < 10⁻¹⁸ eV (PDG), consistent with zero by V-46.
   - Cosmology also needs a non-zero mass of pre-material links: they gravitate and cluster around figure-eights like matter (V-391, V-398).
3. **Consequence: official "dark matter" is the pre-material level.** Each link weighs very little; together they make about 0.26 of the shares (V-391). They are not particles, so direct searches for dark-matter particles will find nothing. In the author's view they will most likely not be detected at the present level of technology. Direct searches so far give zero, which agrees.
4. **Consequence: no free gluons.** A transfer carries an axis of a part and exists only near it (V-343 items 2–3, A-87). This agrees with experiment.
5. **Consequence, testable: no glueballs.** A cycle of transfers only (1→2→3→1) is closed but made of loops, so it is not matter (A-106). There is no hadron of 1.5–2.5 GeV made of transfers only.
   - The glueball candidates f₀(1500), f₀(1710) and X(2370) are, in W, states of parts, that is, of figure-eights.
   - Lattice QCD expects the lightest glueball near 1.7 GeV; it has not been firmly found.
   - If some state is firmly identified as a glueball, W is in trouble.
6. **Agreement.** Massive spin-1 forms (W, Z, ρ, J/ψ) consist of two parts, not of one loop (V-348 item 3). Massive means figure-eights.
7. **Open.** The size of the loop mass is not derived from the postulates.

**V-418 (candidate; the author's mechanism; three conditions from data).** Neutrino oscillation is a change of distinguishability over the three planes at crossings of global figure-eights.
1. **Mechanism (author).** A point, including one self-closed in a figure-eight, is distinguishable in three planes. A neutrino moves along global figure-eights (A-47, V-211). On its way they cross other global figure-eights, and the neutrino's distinguishability over the planes changes.
2. **Agrees.** Three planes, three flavours. From the Z width the number of light flavours is 2.984 ± 0.008 (LEP). Flavour is not a permanent label (V-62, D-18).
3. **Reading.** Only what lags can change. The photon goes exactly with its global figure-eight (no lag, V-46) and keeps its plane: the polarisation of light from distant sources is preserved. The neutrino lags (mass, V-212), slips off its global figure-eight and meets others. In data, flavour change occurs only when masses differ.
4. **Conditions from data.** The mechanism must give:
   - (a) **regularity:** flavour changes periodically and comes back (a wave in L/E: KamLAND, Super-K). Random crossings would mix flavours with no return.
   - (b) **slower change at higher energy:** the phase is ∝ Δm²·L/E. Reading: crossings are counted in the neutrino's own ticks (V-213), and the phase is the difference in the number of closure acts (mass × proper time) between states of different mass.
   - (c) **an effect of matter:** in the Sun and the Earth the change depends on the electron density and acts only on the electron flavour (the MSW effect).
5. **Open.**
   - Why charged leptons do not change flavour in flight (μ → eγ: branching < 1.5·10⁻¹³, MEG). A possible answer: their plane is held by the circuit, and the neutrino has no circuit.
   - What distinguishes the three masses in W.
6. **Dimension of a flavour (author, 28.11; reading).** The electron is one figure-eight, one-dimensional. The muon is a cross, two-dimensional. The tau is three figure-eights, three-dimensional (V-305 item 4). The electron neutrino is distinguishable in one dimension.
   - Among charged leptons, matter contains only electrons (muons and taus decay), that is, one-dimensional figure-eights of the same dimension as the electron flavour. So matter acts only on the electron flavour.
   - This closes condition (c) qualitatively. The size of the effect (its growth with density and energy) is still to be obtained.
7. **Prediction, testable: normal mass ordering.** If the one-dimensional flavour is the lightest, the lightest state is almost entirely electron-like: the electron flavour is about 98 % in the two light states. Data currently favour the normal ordering at 2–3σ; JUNO and DUNE will decide. With inverted ordering this reading fails.
8. **Does not match (open).** The simple map "muon flavour 2D, tau flavour 3D" by mass is not supported by data: the heaviest state is about half muon and half tau, and the middle one is about equal in all three. Above one dimension no match between dimension and mass is found.
9. **Consequence, testable.** At a crossing the neutrino only changes its plane of distinguishability and does not disappear (A-28, A-91). So the shares of flavours in each state, and the shares of states in each flavour, sum to exactly 100 %, not approximately. There are three planes, so there is no fourth ("sterile") neutrino.
   - Check: the total solar flux of all flavours equals what is produced (SNO, D-18).
   - The sum for the electron flavour is tested to a few percent; for muon and tau to 10–20 %.
   - Hints of a fourth neutrino (LSND, MiniBooNE, gallium experiments) were not confirmed later (MicroBooNE, KATRIN, STEREO, PROSPECT), which agrees. If a fourth neutrino is found, the mechanism fails.

**V-419 (candidate; reading from the postulates).** A photon is stopped by matter through the side of its loop. A neutrino passes because the sides of its two loops cancel.
1. **Photon.** Its loop is oriented: it has a side (helicity ±1, D-07).
   - Matter is charges; a charge has a loop direction (A-15), and near a charge the medium carries induced forms (A-87).
   - Each step of the photon is an exchange with a point of the medium (A-19). So in matter it soon meets a charge and is absorbed or scattered.
   - "Mostly": a photon passes through transparent matter when its energy fits no atomic transition.
2. **Neutrino.** A figure-eight in a point: two loops of opposite orientation.
   - The sides cancel (V-34); there is no charge and no magnetic moment (V-56, V-236 item 3), so the charges of matter have nothing to hold.
   - What remains is a direct rebuild when its place coincides with a part of another form (V-209). The neutrino occupies one point, the minimal unit, so such a coincidence is very rare.
   - Crossings with the figure-eights of electrons only change its plane (V-418 item 6); they do not stop it.
3. **Check.** The interaction probability of an MeV neutrino is smaller than a photon's by 10²⁰ times or more; a neutrino passes light-years of lead. This agrees.
4. **Growth with energy (condition from data).** Speed cannot be the cause: it is almost c at any energy (at MeV the difference is below 10⁻¹⁵).
   - From MeV to 10 TeV the probability grows by about 10⁹–10¹⁰ (different targets). It grows in proportion to energy on one target at energies far above the masses. On the nucleon σ/E is constant from about 30 GeV (world average 0.677·10⁻³⁸ cm²/GeV, Formaggio–Zeller 2012). At MeV on the nucleon it grows faster.
   - Reading: energy is transfer (V-209, V-211), and the wavelength gets shorter (A-04). The higher the energy, the more transfer acts per unit path and the more chances to coincide with a part. This would give growth ∝ E; the count is in V-420.
   - Condition: above about 10 TeV the growth slows (officially, the W mass). The W form is a pair of parts (V-348, V-349), and the reading must give the slowdown at that scale. The law of exchange of a part is not derived (V-366).
   - At very high energy (above about 100 TeV) the Earth is opaque to neutrinos (IceCube).
